Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, send me a message and we can talk about it.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After our first conversation, I send you a written proposal with the scope and price, and you decide before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on what you need. The delivery time is part of the proposal, together with the price.

Do I need to know about technology?

No. You explain how you work today and I handle the technical part. At the end, I show you how to use the tool with your own data.

Can we work in English?

Yes. I work with clients in English and Spanish, by message, call or video call.

Will I have to pay monthly fees?

The goal is to avoid costly subscriptions. If a solution needs a paid service, I tell you in the proposal before we start.

What happens to my business information?

Your data stays yours. When the information is sensitive, the solution can run on your own computer or network.

What if I need changes later?

I stay available for adjustments once the tool is running. Bigger changes are quoted separately, always with a clear price.

Do you work with clients outside San José?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses and institutions anywhere in Costa Rica and abroad.

Do you work with public institutions?

Yes. I prepare formal quotes with scope, timeline and price, following each institution's purchasing process. See the public institutions section.

Which task is costing you time or money?

Tell me how you do it today, and let's find a simpler way together.
